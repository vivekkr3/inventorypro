Unified Architecture

Built for Smooth Retail Execution

Purpose-built features designed to eliminate stock variances, accelerate checkouts, and give owners complete financial clarity.

Product Governance

Maintain barcodes/SKUs, multi-category taxonomy, cost & retail selling prices, units, and soft deletion ensuring historical ledger integrity.

Automated Stock Balancing

Inventory counts decrement atomically as cashiers complete invoices. No manual reconciliation, no stock discrepancies.

High-Speed POS Invoicing

Dynamic multi-line billing with instant price lookups, customizable tax rates, discount logic, and print-ready invoices.

Low-Stock Radar

Automatic detection of products below safety thresholds. Topbar pulsing radar beacon and dedicated reorder dashboards prevent stockouts.

Supplier Restock Pipeline

Create supplier purchase orders with cost tracking. Marking an order "Delivered" automatically replenishes product quantities.

Financial P&L Statements

Calculate true Net Profit by subtracting Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and store overhead expenses (rent, utilities, payroll) with interactive charts.

End-to-End Pipeline

How InventoryPro Operates

From supplier purchase order to POS checkout and profit statements.

1
Ingest & Restock

Configure minimum stock safety thresholds. Generate purchase orders to vendors, and mark them delivered to automatically replenish quantities.

2
Fast POS Billing

Cashiers generate customer invoices with multi-item auto-complete, taxes, and discounts. The database automatically decrements item quantities.

3
P&L Intelligence

Review sales velocity, top-selling SKUs, and Net Profit vs. Store Expenses (rent, electricity, salaries) through live interactive dashboards.
